FAQ

Common questions about emergency plumbing

Do you really answer at night and on weekends?

Yes. We’re open 24 hours, 7 days a week. Call 647-609-4297 and you’ll reach us.

How much does an emergency plumber cost?

It depends on the job, so we give you an upfront estimate before any work starts, with no hidden fees. Many jobs come with a free estimate.

What should I do if a pipe bursts?

Shut off your main water supply right away and call us. That limits the water damage until we arrive.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. All of our plumbers are fully licensed, trained and insured, and we’re WSIB covered.

Do you work in condos and commercial buildings?

Yes. We handle condo and multi-residential plumbing, including risers, shut-offs and common areas, and restaurants, offices and retail as well as houses.

How do I pay?

We accept cash, e-transfer and cheque.
